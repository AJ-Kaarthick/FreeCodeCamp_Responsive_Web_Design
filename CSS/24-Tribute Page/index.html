Roger Federer

The Master of Grace, Precision, and Tennis Excellence

Career Highlights & Timeline

  • 1981: Born in Basel, Switzerland.
  • 1998: Wins the Wimbledon Junior title and turns professional.
  • 2003: Claims his first Grand Slam title at Wimbledon against Mark Philippoussis.
  • 2004: Reaches World No. 1 ranking for the first time and wins three Grand Slams in a single year.
  • 2008: Wins Olympic Gold in doubles with Stan Wawrinka in Beijing.
  • 2009: Completes Career Grand Slam by winning the French Open and surpasses Pete Sampras' record with his 15th Major title.
  • 2012: Captures his 7th Wimbledon title and returns to World No. 1.
  • 2017: Makes a historic comeback after injury, winning the Australian Open and an 8th Wimbledon title.
  • 2018: Reaches 20 Grand Slam titles at age 36, becoming the oldest ATP No. 1 in history.
  • 2022: Retires from professional tennis at the Laver Cup after an iconic 24-year career.
